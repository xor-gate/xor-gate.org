## 📋 Ingredient list

* 50 ml cream 30% fat (shelf stable is ok)
* 500 ml water (roomtemp)
* 500 ml cold-brew coffee extract (see chapter [Cold-brew coffee extract](/post/coldbrew-deluxe-v1-recipe/#cold-brew-coffee-extract))
* 3 teaspoons cane sugar (diluted in water) - Around 12,6 grams total
* 20 ml tap water (for the cane sugar concentrate sirup)

This ingredient list dilutes the coffee extract by 1:1 with plain water. Which gives a good taste/caffeine kick. If you want more caffeine kick you can use 2 parts extract on 1 part water. Using only coffee extract is not recommended taste wise and high caffeine content.
## ☕ Cold-brew coffee extract

Cold brew is fantastic because it's less acidic and naturally sweeter than hot-brewed coffee. The base recipe is simple, but there's a lot of room to experiment with ratios and flavors.

This sub-recipe is the "Classic Cold Brew Concentrate" it creates a strong concentrate that you will dilute when serving, which is the standard way to make cold brew.

| Ingredient | Metric | Imperial | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Coffee Grounds** | 125g | 1 cup (whole beans) | Must be **coarsely ground** (like coarse cornmeal or sea salt) |
| **Filtered Cold Water** | 1 Liter | 4 cups | Use filtered water for the best flavor |
| **Ratio (Coffee:Water)** | **1:8** by weight | **1:4** by volume | This is a strong concentrate |
***WARNING***: You may consume this concentrate but be prepared for a panic attack because of the maximum amount of caffeine is extracted from the ground coffee. You will not sleep ;-).
#### 🛠️ Brewing method

1.  **Grind the Coffee:** Grind your coffee beans on the **coarsest setting** possible. This is crucial—too fine a grind will result in cloudy, over-extracted, and gritty coffee.
2.  **Combine & Steep:** Place the coarse grounds in a large container (mason jar, pitcher, or French press). Pour the cold water over the grounds. Stir gently to ensure all the grounds are saturated.
3.  **Wait:** Cover the container and let it steep.
    * **At Room Temperature:** 12 to 15 hours.
    * **In the Refrigerator:** 18 to 24 hours (a slower, gentler extraction).
    * *Tip: Steeping for longer than 24 hours can start to extract bitter flavors.*
4.  **Strain (Filter):** The next day, strain the coffee to separate the grounds from the liquid.
    * **Best Method:** Pour the mixture through a fine-mesh sieve lined with **cheesecloth**, a **nut milk bag**, or a **paper coffee filter**. You may need to do this in batches and filter it twice for a clean brew.
    * **French Press Method:** Slowly push the plunger down. You may still need to filter the final liquid through a paper filter or cheesecloth to remove fine sediment.
5.  **Store:** Transfer the concentrate to an airtight container and refrigerate. It will keep well for up to 1-2 weeks, but the flavor is best in the first week.
#### 🧊 Serving and Diluting

The liquid you made is a **concentrate** and is typically too strong to drink straight. You need to dilute it.

| Dilution Ratio (Concentrate:Liquid) | Resulting Strength |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1:1 Ratio** (Our Recommendation) | Smooth, strong, and balanced |
| **2:1 Ratio** | Very strong (for a big caffeine kick) |
| **1:2 Ratio** | Lighter and less intense |
#### 🥤 Classic Cold Brew Serve

1.  Fill a glass with (crushed) ice.
2.  Add **1 part Cold Brew Concentrate** and **1 part cold water** or **milk** (dairy or non-dairy). An extra variant with a dash of dairy cream makes a dream!
3.  Stir till it splashes and a vortex is seen and enjoy your beverage!

## 🚀 Instructions for the Coldbrew Deluxe Recipe v2

Create cane sugar concentrate (sirup) by boiling the 20 ml water in a water boiler or kettle. Place the 3 teaspoons (or ±12 gram) measured sugar in the tea cup and stir until all granules are dissolved. 

Combine all the ingredients in a vessel and stir with a spoon until uniform. Funnel it into the 1 liter swing top bottle and label with date of creation. Keeps around 1 week in the fridge.
